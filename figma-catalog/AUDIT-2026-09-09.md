# Parity figure audit, 2026-09-09

The question asked: is the 21.5% published on skai.trade/status accurate against
Figma?

**Answer.** The denominator is accurate to the design files as they stood at
12:20 UTC today. The numerator was not: 21.5% counted 601 frames as done on the
strength of status rows alone and ignored the visual-verification tables that
say, frame by frame, whether anyone compared the build to the Figma frame.
Applying those verdicts gives **560 done, 20.0%**, and only **151 of those, 5.4%
of scope**, have been compared to their Figma frame and found to match. The
tally and the status page now carry both figures, and the registry and the
published number can no longer disagree on a frame.

## 1. Denominator: pages

Every page of the three files was re-read through the Plugin API
(`page.loadAsync()`, then `children.length`) at 12:19 to 12:20 UTC and compared
with `live/_pages.json`.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Pages compared | 51 |
| Count mismatches | 0 |
| Pages missing from the manifest | 0 |
| Manifest pages gone from Figma | 0 |
| Live top-level nodes | 5,895 |

The harvest the figure is quoted against is the same day as the measurement,
and nothing has moved under it.

## 2. Denominator: the furniture exclusion

Of 3,336 live top-level nodes on the 32 in-scope pages, 538 are excluded as
furniture and 2,798 counted as genuine frames. The rule is derived from node
type, name and visibility (`coverage.mjs`, section 2), never hand-maintained.

| Why excluded | Nodes |
|---|---|
| Canvas chrome by name (Directory, Breakpoint, Rectangle N, Screenshot, Notes, ...) | 469 |
| Hidden layers | 10 |
| Loose non-frame nodes (18 instances, 10 text, 25 rectangles, 3 vectors, 3 ellipses) | 59 |

Misclassification check: one excluded node carries a screen-style name,
`2736:25840` on Home 1, and it is hidden, so excluding it is right. The genuine
set is 2,780 frames, 17 groups and 1 section. It keeps 167 frames with generic
names (Frame 1000004xxx) and 8 icon-sized frames under 40 px, all of which
enlarge the denominator. The exclusion errs toward a lower percentage, not a
higher one.

## 3. Scope: which pages count

32 pages are in scope. The pages outside the roll-up, with the ruling that put
them there, are listed on the status page itself. The single largest lever on
the figure is Governance and Utilities, `held` by ruling with 659 genuine
frames; counting it would move the denominator to 3,457. Home 1, Wallet 1 and
Trade 1 were ruled in scope today (see `live/_pages.SCOPE-WARNING.md`), which
is why the figure moved from 22.4% to 21.5% before this audit began.

## 4. Numerator: what stood behind 601 done frames

Each of the 601 frames the 21.5% counted as done was joined to its visual
verdict in `vverify.<section>.tsv`, the only evidence in this catalog that a
frame was looked at against Figma.

| Evidence behind a counted `done` | Frames |
|---|---|
| Visual verdict `match` | 146 |
| Visual verdict `partial` | 17 |
| Visual verdict `deferred` | 7 |
| Visual verdict `not-wired` | 4 |
| No visual verdict at all | 427 |

`apply-verify.mjs` already applies these verdicts to `registry.json`, but
`coverage.mjs` tallies from `status.*.tsv` directly and never read them, so the
published percentage and the registry disagreed on 28 frames and the other 427
were counted on a status row alone. That is fixed: `coverage.mjs` section 4b now
applies the same rule per frame (`partial` or `deferred` pulls a claimed `done`
to `partial`; `not-wired` forces `not-started`), and counts `verified`, the done
frames a `match` stands behind.

## 5. Spot checks against Figma

Three frames counted done and verified `match`, all on the Play page at 1440,
were compared by hand: the Figma render against the live app at 1440 by 900.

- **Play hub** (`9099:146637`, `/play`). Hero, Casino and Sports book tiles,
  game search and the game rows all match the frame. The app orders the Sports
  book row before Trending games where Figma has Trending first, and the search
  dropdown reads "All games" where the frame reads "Casino".
- **Casino** (`4711:28434`, `/play/casino`). Hero, filter chips and the
  provider row match. The app omits the Trending games row the frame places
  first and shows one provider where the frame shows three.
- **Resume play** (`4677:40779`, `/play?filter=resume`). Header, search, About
  and FAQ match. The frame draws the populated state with three game cards; the
  audit account has nothing to resume, so the empty state rendered and the
  populated layout could not be exercised.

Conclusion: the `match` verdicts in the verification tables are shell-level,
recording that the route renders the designed structure. A pixel-level pass
would move some of the 151 to `partial`. The 5.4% is an upper bound on
pixel-accurate parity, not a floor.

## 6. What the figure still does not say

- 573 genuine frames carry no status row at all. They count as open, which is
  the honest direction.
- 14 frames carry two same-generation rows that disagree; each is resolved to
  the worse verdict.
- Visual verdicts date from July to September; a verdict is a measurement with
  a date, and a surface can regress after it.

## 7. Recommended next

A visual verification wave over the 409 done frames nobody has looked at,
largest first: Wallet 2 (125), Trade 2 (88), Social (60), Home 2 (48), Play
(42). Each row lands in `vverify.<section>.tsv` and is applied on the next
`npm run catalog`; the figure will move on its own.
